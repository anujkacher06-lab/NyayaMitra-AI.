1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Millions of Indian citizens encounter cyber-financial scams, arbitrary rent
deductions, delayed salaries, or rejected e-commerce refunds every month. Most
struggle to navigate the legal system due to heavy legal jargon, confusion over
which police station or portal to approach, lack of evidence preservation, and
fear of high advocate fees.

NyayaMitra AI is a verified first-step civic legal guidance assistant designed to
bridge the pre-litigation gap. It provides plain-language statutory explanations,
immediate action checklists with official helplines (1930 / 1915 / 15100), itemized
evidence preservation lists, customized complaint drafting, and direct routing
to State Legal Services Authorities (SLSA) and NALSA.

Crucially, NyayaMitra AI enforces strict guardrails:
 - No hallucinated court precedents or fabricated statutory sections.
 - Replaced outdated colonial IPC/CrPC citations with modern 2023-2024 laws:
   * Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)
   * Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)
   * Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)
 - Explicit disclaimers clarifying that guidance is procedural legal information,
   not formal lawyer-client representation.


2. CORE CIVIC DOMAINS GROUNDED IN VERIFIED STATUTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Cyber & Financial Fraud:
   - Covers: UPI scams, fake payment links, OTP fraud, unauthorized debits,
     KYC extortion, SIM swap, impersonation, loan app harassment.
   - Statutory Grounding:
     * Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 66C - Identity Theft, 66D - Cheating by Personation).
     * Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Section 318 - Cheating, Section 319 - Cheating by Personation).
     * Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (Section 63 - Electronic Records Admissibility).
   - Portals & Helplines: National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930), cybercrime.gov.in.

2. Consumer Complaints & Disputes:
   - Covers: Defective electronics, denied refunds, transit damages, false ads,
     e-commerce deficiencies, unfair trade practices.
   - Statutory Grounding:
     * Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Section 2(7) - Consumer Rights, Section 35 - District Commission filing).
     * Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
   - Portals & Helplines: National Consumer Helpline (NCH 1915), INGRAM, and e-Daakhil
     (zero court fees for consumer claims up to ₹5 Lakh).

3. Workplace & Salary Issues:
   - Covers: Unpaid wages, withheld Full & Final (F&F) settlements, non-issuance
     of relieving letters, illegal pay cuts, non-deposit of PF/gratuity.
   - Statutory Grounding:
     * Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (Section 5 - Time of payment, Section 15 - Claims).
     * Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
     * Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.
   - Portals & Helplines: Ministry of Labour Samadhan Portal, Labour Commissioner, EPFiGMS.

4. Tenancy & Rental Disputes:
   - Covers: Unjustified security deposit withholding, illegal eviction threats,
     arbitrary power/water utility cutoffs, unauthorized lock changes.
   - Statutory Grounding:
     * Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (recommends max 2-month residential deposit cap; central model
       framework adopted by States).
     * Transfer of Property Act, 1882 (Section 106 - Mandatory 15 to 30 days written notice).
     * Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (Section 316 - Criminal Breach of Trust, Section 329 - Criminal Trespass).
   - Portals & Helplines: Local Rent Authority / Rent Tribunal, DLSA, Tele-Law (14488).

5. RTI (Right to Information) Assistance:
   - Covers: Delayed government scholarships, pending pensions, municipal project
     budgets, official grievance file tracking.
   - Statutory Grounding:
     * Right to Information Act, 2005 (Section 6(1) - Application, Section 7(1) - 30-day mandate,
       Section 19(1) - First Appeal within 30 days of default).
   - Portals & Helplines: rtionline.gov.in (statutory fee ₹10, free for BPL applicants),
     Central Information Commission (CIC).


3. KEY FEATURES IMPLEMENTED & ARCHITECTURAL HIGHLIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Multi-Tiered OCR Document & Transaction Receipt Scanner (/scan-document):
   - Tier 1 (Cloud Vision): Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision OCR transcribes and extracts
     structured dispute entities with high accuracy from UPI screenshots (PhonePe,
     Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, YONO), bank debit alerts, eviction letters, and bills.
   - Tier 2: OpenAI GPT-4o-mini Vision OCR integration.
   - Tier 3: Local Pytesseract OCR (when Tesseract binary is available on the system).
   - Tier 4 (Offline / Zero-Key): Pillow Image Inspector validates image format (PNG,
     JPEG, WebP, BMP), dimensions, file integrity, extracts metadata, and parses
     plaintext transaction logs with regex.
   - Structured Entity Extraction:
     * Transaction / Claim Amount (e.g., ₹14,500)
     * 12-Digit Bank / UPI UTR Reference Number (e.g., 412345678901)
     * Incident Date (DD/MM/YYYY)
     * Payment App / Channel (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, Bank Netbanking)
     * Auto-detected legal category
     * Auto-generated bilingual dispute summary for 1-click form pre-filling.

B. Interactive Web UI & Glassmorphism Design:
   - Modern, high-contrast dark/gold aesthetic with responsive layout.
   - Bilingual State Engine (English & Hindi) covering headings, labels, sample
     prompts, form placeholders, and toast notifications.
   - Camera OCR Upload button with live scanning animations, entity chips preview,
     and auto-fill prompt integration.
   - Web Speech API integration for hands-free voice dictation in Hindi & English.

C. State-Wise Legal Aid Locator (19 States & Union Territories):
   - Comprehensive directory mapping in data/state_legal_aid.json covering:
     Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal,
     Gujarat, Rajasthan, Telangana, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana,
     Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Assam, Jharkhand, and National Default.
   - Returns State Legal Services Authority (SLSA) address, toll-free helpline (15100),
     State Cyber Wing portals, State Consumer Commissions, and free legal aid income
     eligibility caps (e.g. ₹3 Lakh in Delhi/Maharashtra).

D. Customizable Complaint Draft Generator & Print/Export:
   - Pre-fills formal legal representations with applicant's name, respondent's name,
     transaction amount, UTR, date, and factual timeline.
   - Formatted A4 printable view at /print-draft ready for physical submission or PDF saving.
   - Plaintext Citizen Legal Dossier download (/download-dossier/<id>) for offline archiving.
   - Itemized Evidence Preservation Checklist download (/download-checklist/<id>) citing
     BSA 2023 Section 63 electronic record certification guidelines.

E. Interactive Follow-up Legal Chat (/follow-up):
   - Context-aware procedural assistant answering citizen queries.
   - Specific statutory guidance for police FIR refusal under BNSS 2023 Section 173(4)
     (written representation to SP/DCP by registered post) and Section 175(3) (Magistrate inquiry).
   - Pre-litigation statutory fee structures (e.g. ₹0 for cybercrime & CPA <₹5 Lakh, ₹10 for RTI).

F. High-Efficiency Caching & Strict User Privacy Isolation:
   - In-memory LRU caching (@lru_cache) eliminates repetitive file I/O for statutory datasets.
   - SQLite Response Cache (`response_cache`) computes SHA-256 normalized hash keys returning
     verified legal responses in <15ms.
   - STRICT PRIVACY GUARANTEE: User-identifying attributes (user_name, state, query_id)
     are stripped before storing in cache, guaranteeing zero cross-citizen data leakage.

G. Security Hardening:
   - Header-based API key authentication (x-goog-api-key) prevents credentials from leaking
     into web server access logs or URL parameters.
   - Safe dynamic secret key generation (os.urandom(24).hex()) if SECRET_KEY is not set.
   - Safe debug flags: Flask debug mode defaults to False unless FLASK_DEBUG=true.
